# 🏭 Manufacturing & Field Engineering Documentation Portal

Welcome to the central technical documentation repository for plant operations, equipment assembly, and field service workflows. This portal maintains version-controlled Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), Work Instructions (WIs), and Engineering Change Logs.


## 👥 Stakeholder Alignment

This repository serves cross-functional teams across the equipment lifecycle:

| Stakeholder Group | Primary Scope & Documentation Access |
|---|---|
| **Industrial Engineering** | Line balancing, takt time validation, ergonomic evaluations, and tooling specifications. |
| **Design / R&D** | Design for Manufacturing & Assembly (DFMA) review, tolerances, and CAD cross-referencing. |
| **Production Support & Operators** | Step-by-step Standard Work Instructions (SWIs), safety checklists, and acceptance criteria. |
| **Field Service Engineers** | Remote/on-site troubleshooting procedures, field replacement, and recovery routines. |
| **Supply Chain & Quality** | Bill of Materials (BOM), spare part tracking numbers, and incoming inspection criteria. |
